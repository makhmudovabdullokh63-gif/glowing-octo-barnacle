About ESP

English for Specific Purposes Certification

English for Specific Purposes focuses on language used in a particular academic, professional or practical context. Instead of learning only general English, learners work with vocabulary, communication and tasks connected to their purpose.

ESP With Me brings these materials together in a focused digital experience so learners can find the resources relevant to their goals.

FAQ

Questions about ESP

What does ESP mean?

ESP means English for Specific Purposes: learning English for a particular academic, professional or practical context.

Who is ESP for?

ESP can be useful for learners who need English connected to a specific field, role, study area or professional situation.

How do I find a suitable course book?

Start with the course-book section and choose a resource based on the context and communication needs it describes.
